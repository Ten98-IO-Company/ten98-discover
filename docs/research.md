# Source research and parity baseline

This is an evidence-backed plan, not a claim of implemented or tested parity. Research date: 2026-10-12. The first usable/public ten98-monitor release must cover the union of the capabilities below. Internal milestones may be staged; they do not permit silently moving capabilities out of V1. [The parity register](parity.md) makes the planned acceptance requirements explicit.

## Method and evidence status

This follows REA's source-repository route: inventory entrypoints, inspect contracts and schemas, trace representative operations through authorization, persistence and background work, then distinguish observed behavior from architectural inference and unknowns. It is static research of pinned source trees. No upstream application or paid-provider call was executed during this audit. A route or implementation establishes an observed capability, not a passing runtime test. Filenames alone were not treated as proof of advanced functionality such as SAML or a publishing CMS.

- **Observed:** present in the inspected pinned source, with evidence below.
- **Inferred:** a proposed ten98-monitor design or an implication of the source.
- **Unknown:** requires runtime tests, provider access, deployment-version matching or user confirmation.

All source references use immutable commit URLs. The selected revisions are a parity baseline, not a promise to match every future upstream release. Freeze a new baseline explicitly when updating it.
